About these rates and your privacy

Toll rates come from the latest Toll Regulatory Board matrices, checked on 17 November 2026, with VAT included: NLEX and SCTEX from 20 January 2026, Skyway and SLEX from 1 July 2026, STAR from 1 July 2026, CAVITEX from 9 May 2026 and CALAX from 9 August 2026. Car rates are exact. Bus and truck rates are exact on Skyway, SLEX, STAR and CALAX, and estimates elsewhere. The rate posted at the toll plaza is always the final amount.

Places you search and your route points are sent to OpenStreetMap, Photon and OSRM to find places and draw the route. Your recent trips and theme are saved only on this device.

Wrong toll or plaza? Email tollcheckph@gmail.com.
